How Authenticus Works

A seamless four-step process that transforms product authentication from a challenge into a competitive advantage.

1. Secure Tagging

Embed tamper-proof NFC tags during manufacturing with unique cryptographic identifiers.

2. Seamless Scanning

Consumers tap with any smartphone - no app required - for instant verification.

3. Proactive Engagement

Provide full product information and engagement links or integrate with your own platforms.

4. Actionable Insights

Real-time analytics on authentication patterns and potential counterfeit activity.
